## Why it doesn't burn your eyes

Most cyberpunk themes are a screenshot of Night City poured into your editor:
pure #00f0ff everywhere, magenta keywords, straight #000 background. _Maybe_ gorgeous
in a repo thumbnail, unusable by lunch. Edgerunners is built from the anime's
actual color logic instead: night first, neon rare, and every loud color
earning its place.

Frame palettes of the show come back to five colors: acid yellow #f8e602,
toxic green #4bff21, cyan #00f0ff, pale pink #f4d5fd, deep purple #772289.
The theme keeps four and tames them before they reach your eyes.
